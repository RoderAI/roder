# Schedule reminders and recurring follow-ups in the current thread

Agents can subscribe to named topics, schedule one-shot reminders or recurring
checks, inspect their next delivery, and stop watchers when the requested work
finishes. Follow-ups retain the conversation and wait until the thread is idle.
These subscriptions live in the running harness; durable independent schedules
remain available through app-server automations.
